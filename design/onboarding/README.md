## Rebuild

`python scripts/onboarding-images.py` turns `src/*.png` into tintable WebP files:
- a white alpha mask; hairlines are lifted with a 0.45 gamma, and speckles under 10 alpha are dropped;
- heroes are 1080 px wide and spots are 384 px.

It also writes `contact-sheet.png` (every image on all six look backgrounds) for review.

In Compose, draw them with `Image(painterResource(R.drawable.onboarding_welcome), null, colorFilter = ColorFilter.tint(MaterialTheme.colorScheme.primary))`.

`drafts/` keeps the alternatives that weren't chosen.
