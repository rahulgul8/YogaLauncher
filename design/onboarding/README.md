## Reels prompt

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: premium Android onboarding illustration, final landscape PNG at 1536×1024
Primary request: Create a short strip of photographic film coiled into a loose, open loop that is visibly coming apart, with one free end falling away downward in a gentle relaxed curve.
Input images: welcome.png, shorts.png, and done.png are style references only; match their restrained scale, fine line weight, generous negative space, and calm engraved character. In particular, follow shorts.png for unmistakable film-strip construction and edge perforations.
Scene/backdrop: fully transparent canvas, no paper, no scene, no shadow.
Subject: one short strip of film. Both long edges carry a regular row of small rectangular sprocket perforations throughout the visible strip. The loop must remain visibly open and asymmetrical, with overlapping film segments and an unmistakable loose free end, so the object reads immediately as physical film uncoiling—not as a closed outlined symbol.
Style/medium: pure black (#000000) hairline etched engraving line art, like a luxury watch catalogue or banknote technical engraving; sparse delicate cross-hatching only where needed to clarify overlap and ribbon curvature; no solid fills and no grey wash.
Composition/framing: landscape 3:2 canvas; subject centered and confined to roughly the middle 55–60% of the canvas; abundant empty space on all sides. Gentle balanced diagonal flow. The falling free end curves down and away from the open loop.
Lighting/mood: calm, quiet, precise, premium.
Color palette: black linework only with genuine alpha transparency everywhere else.
Constraints: output 1536×1024 PNG; fully transparent background; perforations along both film edges; no text, letters, numbers, logos, UI, or watermark; no people; preserve lots of negative space.
Avoid: any closed speech-bubble silhouette, speech-bubble tail, heart shape, circle-with-tail symbol, app icon, Instagram/Reels mark, YouTube mark, play triangle, camera glyph, logo-like geometry, opaque white or off-white background, paper texture, vignette, solid black regions, thick outlines, busy composition.
```

## Rebuild

`python scripts/onboarding-images.py` turns `src/*.png` into tintable WebP files:
- a white alpha mask; hairlines are lifted with a 0.45 gamma, and speckles under 10 alpha are dropped;
- heroes are 1080 px wide and spots are 384 px.

It also writes `contact-sheet.png` (every image on all six look backgrounds) for review.

In Compose, draw them with `Image(painterResource(R.drawable.onboarding_welcome), null, colorFilter = ColorFilter.tint(MaterialTheme.colorScheme.primary))`.

`drafts/` keeps the alternatives that weren't chosen.
