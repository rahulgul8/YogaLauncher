## Why

App and category allowances currently notify but do not restrict use. People also need a deliberate, scarce way through a limit or mindful pause for urgent tasks.

## What Changes

- Enforce app and category allowances at Yoga launches and from Yoga guard.
- Add three five-minute urgent opens per local day, started by a three-second hold.
- After an urgent window, use greyscale when available or five-minute reminders until the app is left.
- Show urgent usage in Usage, back up daily counts, and explain greyscale availability.

## Impact

- Affected specs: usage-limits
- Affected code: usage rules and notifications, launcher gating, mindful-pause UI, Yoga guard, greyscale reconciliation, backups
- No dependency or Room schema change

Approval: Brief 23 directly requests this proposal and implementation.
