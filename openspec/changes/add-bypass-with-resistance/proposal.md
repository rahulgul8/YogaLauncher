## Why

A hard Shorts/Reels block makes people disable the entire guard when they want a way through. An instant bypass encourages reflexive continuation. A growing pause and a deliberate, bounded choice keep the guard useful.

## What Changes

- J1: family-scoped timed passes, local passes/resisted counters, strict preference and tomorrow-effective strict-off date; reuse ResistanceLadder.
- J2: a quiet Watch anyway block-card action, breathing countdown, duration choices, hold to continue, accessible activation, Not now, expiry message and free pass pill.
- J3: apply growing waits, chips, hold, cost/resisted counts and a time-up nudge to the launcher app pause.
- J4: settings resistance for loosening rules and strict mode; strict changes take effect tomorrow, tightening stays immediate.
- All resistance features are free. Pauses for apps opened outside Yoga (J5) remain deferred until after first Play approval.

## Impact

- Affected specs: yoga-guard; J3 will add its app-pause delta in its own implementation.
- Affected code: guard models/preferences/gate/service, ResistActivity, ResistScreen, guard manifest; later focus pause and settings.
- No dependencies, detection changes, content collection or network transmission.

Approval: the user's brief19 implementation request authorizes J1 and J2. J3 and J4 are documented scope, not implemented in this worktree.
