03 · Intention

A breath between you and the feed.

Before a distracting app opens, Yoga asks for a few seconds and an optional reason. Most of the time, that is all it takes to put the phone down.

  • Mindful pause

    A short countdown and a "What for?" before the apps you choose. Your recent reasons come back as one-tap chips.

  • TimeboxPro

    Give a session a boundary. A gentle note when it ends, on the minute.

  • Focus and schedulesPro

    Tuck distracting apps away and turn on Do Not Disturb, now or on a schedule.

  • Greyscale, on your terms

    Over the limit, during focus or at bedtime. Colour returns when you are done.

  • Quiet notificationsPro

    Hold notifications from the apps you pick, then read them together in a calm digest.

  • Yoga guard

    Optional. Turn off YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels, or give each a daily allowance. Regular videos stay.

07 · Questions

Before you switch.

Anything else, write to tuwi.developer@gmail.com. A person reads every message.

Which phones does Yoga work on?

Any Android phone running Android 8.0 or later. Yoga is not available for iPhone.

Can I go back to my old home screen?

Yes, at any time. Open your phone's Settings, then Apps, Default apps, Home app, and choose the one you had before. Uninstalling Yoga does the same and removes all of its data.

What can Yoga see on my phone?

Which apps you use, and for how long, from Android's usage data. If you turn on quiet notifications, Yoga holds the title and text of notifications from the apps you choose, on your phone, for at most a day. If you enable Yoga guard, it checks screen structure only in YouTube, Instagram and the browsers you allow, to recognise Shorts and Reels; that content is never stored or sent. Yoga never sees your contacts, location, photos, microphone or camera.

What happens when the free trial ends?

Your home screen, search, dock, one allowance, the mindful pause and Shorts and Reels blocking keep working, free. Pro features pause and keep your settings, so buying later brings everything back as it was.

Will Yoga slow my phone or drain the battery?

Yoga is built to be light: plain text instead of icon grids, no background network traffic, and no polling when Yoga guard is off. It reads usage from Android's own records rather than watching constantly.
